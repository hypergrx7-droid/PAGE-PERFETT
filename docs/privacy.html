Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026
Short version: PagePerfect collects no data. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no internet permission. Every scan, image and PDF stays in the app's private storage on your device.

1. Who we are

PagePerfect is a free, open-source document scanner application for Android. This policy covers the PagePerfect Android application and this website.

2. Data we collect

None. Specifically:

CategoryCollected?
Name, email, phone numberNo
Account or loginNo — there is no account system
Photos, documents, scanned filesNo — stored only on your device
LocationNo
ContactsNo
Device identifiers / advertising IDNo
Usage analytics or crash reportsNo
Cookies (app)No

3. Permissions and why

  • Camera — required to scan documents. Frames are processed on-device in memory.
  • Photos / media (read) — only if you choose to import an existing image.
  • Storage (write) — only to save the PDF or image you asked for.

The application does not declare the INTERNET permission. Without it, the operating system blocks all network access at the kernel level — the app cannot transmit data even if it wanted to.

4. Where your documents live

  • Scans and PDFs are written to the app's private storage directory.
  • If you use Share or Export, the file goes only where you send it (another app, email, cloud drive of your choosing).
  • Uninstalling the app removes its private storage. Files you exported elsewhere are unaffected.

5. Third parties

The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no third-party tracking library. The only third-party components are open-source libraries listed in the project's THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md file, none of which transmit data.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or stored by us, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Deleting the app deletes all app data from your device.

8. Distribution and updates

The APK is distributed through GitHub Releases. Downloading from GitHub is subject to GitHub's privacy statement. This website is hosted on a static host and sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the "last updated" date above will change and the diff will be visible in the public source repository.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/hypergrx7-droid/PAGE-PERFETT/issues.
